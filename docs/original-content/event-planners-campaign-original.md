# Be Seen Event Planning Meta Campaign

Prepared: June 26, 2026  
Brand: Be Seen Print Sign and Design  
Campaign focus: Event planning services for Facebook and Instagram  
Landing page: https://getbeseen.com/events/

## Campaign Positioning

Be Seen helps Bay Area event planners, coordinators, venues, businesses, nonprofits, couples, and hosts turn an event idea into ready-to-use materials: event signage, printed pieces, promotional giveaways, QR codes, and event web pages.

Core promise:

> One local partner for the printed, branded, and digital details that make an event feel organized, polished, and ready for guests.

Primary call to action:

> Request an event quote.

Best supporting message:

> Bring the event list. Be Seen will help organize the production list.

## Launch Recommendation

Start with a lead-generation campaign, not a pure traffic campaign.

The landing page currently has a placeholder form area for GoHighLevel. Until that form is embedded and tested, use Meta instant forms for paid lead generation. Once the GoHighLevel form and Meta tracking are live, shift the strongest ads to the landing page and optimize for completed quote requests.

Recommended first 30 days:

| Campaign | Objective | Budget Share | Purpose |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Event Quote Requests | Leads | 70% | Generate quote requests from local planners, venues, hosts, businesses, and nonprofits. |
| Event Planning Retargeting | Leads or Traffic | 20% | Re-engage people who watched videos, opened forms, clicked ads, or engaged with Facebook/Instagram profiles. |
| Organic Boost Tests | Engagement or Leads | 10% | Boost the best-performing organic event posts after they show early engagement. |

Starter daily budget:

| Budget Level | Daily Budget | Use When |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Lean test | $25-$35/day | You want proof of message before scaling. |
| Practical local launch | $50-$75/day | You want enough delivery to compare the three event lanes. |
| Faster learning | $100-$150/day | You have form tracking ready and can respond quickly to leads. |

## Creative Concepts

### Concept 1: One Local Event Partner

Visual:

Show a coordinated event kit: welcome sign, invitation or program, tote/tumbler giveaway, QR code sign, and event website mockup.

Best formats:

Feed static, Story static, Reel with quick cuts.

Copy direction:

> Planning an event in the Bay Area? Be Seen helps coordinate the printed, branded, and digital details through one local team.

### Concept 2: From Guest Arrival to Follow-Up

Visual:

Carousel with one card per event moment: Arrival, Check-In, Navigation, Sponsor Visibility, Guest Takeaways, Digital Follow-Through.

Best formats:

Carousel, slideshow Reel.

Copy direction:

> From the first welcome sign to the last thank-you note, keep every event detail connected.

### Concept 3: Sponsor-Ready Events

Visual:

Conference or fundraiser setup with sponsor board, step-and-repeat backdrop, QR donation sign, badges, lanyards, and giveaway table.

Best formats:

Feed static, Reel, Facebook post.

Copy direction:

> Give sponsors and guests a clear, polished experience with signs, print, promos, QR codes, and event pages.

### Concept 4: Wedding Details That Match

Visual:

Wedding invitation suite next to welcome sign, seating chart, table number, menu, favor, and RSVP/wedding website screen.

Best formats:

Instagram carousel, Reel, Story.

Copy direction:

> Match the details from save-the-date to welcome sign with one local team.

### Concept 5: Bring the Event List

Visual:

Planner checklist beside finished materials: signs, programs, badges, giveaways, QR codes, website.

Best formats:

Feed static, retargeting ad, Story.

Copy direction:

> Send the date, guest count, items needed, quantities, and artwork. Be Seen will help turn it into a practical production list.

## Tracking and Setup Checklist

Before launch:

- Replace the landing page placeholder form with the live GoHighLevel form.
- Confirm the form asks for event type, event date, items needed, quantities, design help, giveaway needs, website needs, and artwork upload.
- Add the real Google Tag Manager ID in the landing page.
- Add Meta Pixel through GTM or GoHighLevel.
- Track these actions: PageView, landing page click, phone click, email click, form start, and completed lead.
- Test UTM capture into hidden form fields.
- Create a CRM tag: Event Coordinator Lead.
- Create follow-up automation for form submissions and missed calls.

Suggested UTM structure:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| utm_source | meta |
| utm_medium | paid_social |
| utm_campaign | event_planning_launch |
| utm_content | audience_format_concept_version |
| utm_term | optional audience or creative hook |

Example URL:

https://getbeseen.com/events/?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=event_planning_launch&utm_content=corporate_feed_sponsor_ready_v1

## Optimization Plan

Review after 3 days:

- Confirm ads are approved.
- Check spend, delivery, CPM, CTR, landing page views, form opens, leads, and CPL.
- Look for obvious creative fatigue or poor placement fit.
- Confirm leads are arriving in GoHighLevel or Meta Lead Center.

Review after 7 days:

- Pause ads with high spend and no meaningful engagement.
- Move budget toward the audience lane with the best lead quality, not just the cheapest CPL.
- Compare corporate, wedding, and fundraiser leads by event date, budget potential, and response quality.
- Add retargeting if there is enough traffic or engagement.

Review after 14 days:

- Refresh the weakest creative concept.
- Duplicate the strongest concept into the missing format, usually Story/Reel if Feed worked first.
- Test one offer-specific variant: "Free event production checklist" or "Request an event quote."

## Compliance Notes

- Keep copy focused on services and event needs instead of implying personal attributes.
- Avoid claims that sound guaranteed, such as "stress-free event" or "perfect event."
- Avoid using third-party logos, venue logos, sponsor logos, or trademarked product images unless Be Seen has permission.
- If ads show alcohol-related event imagery, keep the campaign age targeting and copy compliant with local requirements.
- Make sure the landing page matches the ad promise and has a working way to submit contact details before spending heavily.
