Game Rules

  • One player reads while the other two compete.
  • The full question must be read before anyone slaps.
  • First slap gets the first answer.
  • Correct answer: +1 point.
  • Wrong answers escalate separately for each player: first miss -1, second miss -2, third miss -3, and so on.
  • After a wrong first answer, the other player gets one chance without slapping.
  • First player to reach 15 net points wins.
  • Questions come from one shared 1,000-question pool and continue forward even after Reset Game.
